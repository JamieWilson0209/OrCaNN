group analysis output: figures and tables (one aggregate run, not per-recording)

  analysis_outputs_<run key>/
      data/     analysis_results.json  dataset_features.csv  selected_rois.csv
                quality_gating.json  outlier_report.txt
      figures/  grouped QC, genotype and developmental-day comparison figures
      run_info.json

Aggregates one activity store. Having no recording of its own, its key carries
the cohort -- every recording it read, with the digest of that recording's
activity record -- so one recording being reprocessed makes these figures stale
and `orcann status` names which recording changed. The record is written only
when every stage of the analysis completed.

Written by: analysis.

upload to GitHub. Contents stay local and are not committed; delete this placeholder
