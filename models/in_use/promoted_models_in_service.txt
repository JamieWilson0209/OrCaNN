promoted models the pipeline may run

  <name>_<UTC timestamp>_<4 hex>/
      segmenter.pt   the model, verified against the digest recorded inside it

models.spatial in config.yaml selects one by identity, or `latest` for the newest
by timestamp. `latest` is resolved afresh on every run and the resolved identity
is what gets recorded, never the word itself.

A model arrives here only by promotion, never by finishing a training run.
