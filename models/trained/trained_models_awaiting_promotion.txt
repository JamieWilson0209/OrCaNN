      segmenter.pt        the model, with its identity and content digest inside

A finished training run writes here and stops. Nothing reads a model from this
folder: putting one into service is a separate act, so that choosing a model
appears in the shell history of whoever chose it.

  orcann models --config config.yaml                     list what is here
  orcann models --config config.yaml --promote <identity>   copy it into service
