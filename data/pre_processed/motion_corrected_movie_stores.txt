motion-corrected movies, one store per settings choice

  motion_correction_outputs_<run key>/
      <recording>.tif          the corrected movie
      <recording>.json         shift summary; also what marks the recording done
      <recording>_shifts.npy   per-frame [dy, dx], for the analysis motion QC

The store is named for the stage and `run.key`. What decides whether this output
is reused is not the name but the key recorded in each <recording>.json: the
settings the stage ran under and the tag of the raw file it read. Change a
setting and those recordings read as out of date and are redone; change the run
key alone and this store is found and added to, rather than copied.

Movies corrected outside this pipeline go in the root of this folder rather than
in a store, and the pipeline starts at infer. They are identified by content the
same way raw recordings are; having no motion record, they carry no motion QC
into the analysis stage, which excludes them for unknown motion rather than
treating them as still.
