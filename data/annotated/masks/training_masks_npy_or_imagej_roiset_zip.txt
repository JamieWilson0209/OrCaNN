training masks (to train the spatial segmenter)

Pairs with the same-stem movie in ../movies/:
  <stem>.npy     integer instance-label image
  <stem>.zip     ImageJ RoiSet.zip

<stem> must equal the movie's exactly. ImageJ saves a set under a decorated name
(blind_0001_allroi.zip); that is a different stem and pairs with nothing, so
rename it to blind_0001.zip, or run rasterize_rois, which names its output after
the movie it matched.
